03 / ROADMAP

Earn autonomy in stages.

A good agent launch is a product progression, not a switch you flip.

STAGE 01

Observe

Instrument the workflow and establish baselines for time, errors, escalations, and success.

Exit gate: trusted baseline
STAGE 02

Assist

Agent retrieves context, summarizes state, and proposes next steps without taking action.

Exit gate: ≥90% useful recommendations
STAGE 03

Recommend

Agent creates structured action plans with confidence, evidence, and clear human approval.

Exit gate: target precision + review speed
STAGE 04

Act

Execute approved actions through typed tools, with full traceability and rollback.

Exit gate: sustained reliability
